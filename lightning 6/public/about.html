Private by design

Your work stays with you unless you decide to share it.

Stored on your device

Chats, projects, images and artifacts are saved in your own browser. We don’t keep a copy on a server.

Keys stay protected

Keys you paste are kept in your browser. Keys the site owner adds live in server settings and are never sent to your page.

Sharing is opt-in

Nothing is public until you press “Publish artifact”. Published pages run in a locked-down sandbox, and you can delete yours any time.

Questions

Is lightning free?

lightning itself is free and open source. The AI models it connects to have their own free tiers and limits, and some models cost money. You choose which to use.

Which AI models does it use?

Whatever you connect: models from OpenRouter, Groq and Google Gemini, or any OpenAI-compatible service. Coder mode prefers a coding-focused model.

Where are my chats stored?

In your browser’s local storage on this device. Clearing your browser data removes them, so download anything you want to keep.

What happens when a model is rate limited?

lightning tries other models, then switches to the next connected provider, and tells you what it’s doing.

Can I share what I build?

Yes. Open an artifact and press “Publish artifact” to get a public link. You can also download it as a file.

Is lightning affiliated with OpenAI or another AI company?

No. lightning is an independent project. Model names belong to their respective owners.
